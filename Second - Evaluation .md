# Second Example: Evaluating an AI-Generated Response

## Scientific Question

What is the role of DNA in living organisms?

## AI-Generated Response

DNA contains genetic information that provides instructions for the development, functioning, and reproduction of living organisms.

## Evaluation

### Accuracy
The response is scientifically accurate.

### Completeness
The response correctly describes the central role of DNA as the carrier of genetic information. A more detailed answer could also mention DNA replication and inheritance.

### Clarity
The response is concise and easy to understand.

### Overall Assessment
The response provides a scientifically appropriate explanation.

## Evaluation Criteria

- Scientific accuracy
- Completeness
- Clarity
- Relevance
- Unsupported claims
